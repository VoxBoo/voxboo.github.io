How It Works

1. Activate

Press Option-Space from anywhere to enter voice mode. Speak freely for dictation or commands.

2. Command

Use built-in actions or custom skills. Say "play jazz on Spotify" or "add lunch with Alex tomorrow."

3. AI Processes

Your chosen LLM (OpenAI, Claude, etc.) handles complex tasks like screen analysis or translations locally or via your API key.

4. Done

Results appear instantly—in apps, clipboard, or notifications. Switch modes with Option-Q anytime.

Yes. Memory and processing stay on your Mac. No cloud required unless you choose an external LLM.
